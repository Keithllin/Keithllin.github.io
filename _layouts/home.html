Research focus

Physical intelligence for assistive systems

Assistive robotics

Robot perception and manipulation for caregiving, bedding, cloth, and human-centered physical interaction.

Visual-tactile perception

Combining vision and contact sensing to reason about transparent, deformable, and difficult-to-observe objects.

Accessibility AI

Sign-language translation systems and dictionary-grounded interfaces for communication access.

I am an M.S. student at Carnegie Mellon University and a member of the Robotic Caregiving and Human Interaction Lab, advised by Zackory Erickson. I received my B.S. in Aerospace Engineering from Sun Yat-sen University, where I worked on robotic perception and manipulation with the Robotics and Intelligent Sensing Lab.
